MODULE 05: REGULATORY (CHINA-SPECIFIC)
================================================================================

CHINA MARKET
- Development Approval: NDRC (National Development and Reform Commission)
- Grid Connection: State Grid approval required (6-12 months)
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA): Required for all >50MW projects
- Land Use: Collective land lease or state-owned land transfer
- FiT Policy: Feed-in-Tariff guaranteed for 20 years (now transitioning to competitive bidding)

CROSS-BORDER TOKEN ISSUANCE
- Use Cayman SPV for token issuance (avoid direct China securities regulation)
- WFOE (Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise) owns wind farm
- Profit repatriation via dividends (subject to 10% withholding tax)
- Hong Kong SFC license required if selling tokens to HK investors

MODULE 07: RISKS & MITIGATION
================================================================================

WIND-SPECIFIC RISKS
1. **Wind Resource Variability** (HIGH IMPACT)
   - Mitigation: P50/P75/P90 wind studies, 3+ years of wind data, insurance
   
2. **Turbine Failure** (MEDIUM IMPACT)
   - Mitigation: OEM warranty (5-10 years), spare parts inventory, O&M contract with manufacturer

3. **Grid Curtailment** (CHINA-SPECIFIC, HIGH IMPACT)
   - Risk: Grid operator curtails wind power during low-demand periods
   - Mitigation: Site selection in low-curtailment provinces (Jiangsu, Hebei better than Gansu, Xinjiang)
   - Compensation: Some provinces offer curtailment compensation

4. **Blade Damage** (typhoons, icing)
   - Mitigation: Insurance, blade de-icing systems, typhoon cut-out controls

5. **PPA Counterparty Risk**
   - Low risk in China (State Grid AAA-rated)
   - Medium risk in Southeast Asia (depends on utility creditworthiness)
