1. TECHNICAL PARAMETERS FOR 50MW SOLAR PROJECTS
================================================

CAPITAL EXPENDITURE (CAPEX)
---------------------------
Parameter                    Typical Range          Notes
Total Capex per Watt        $0.55 - $0.75/W        Depends on region and EPC pricing
Total Project Capex (50MW)  $27.5M - $37.5M       Complete turnkey installation
- Solar Panels              40-45% of total        High-efficiency monocrystalline preferred
- Inverters                 8-10% of total         Central vs string inverter choice
- Mounting Structure        12-15% of total        Fixed tilt vs tracker systems
- Balance of System         15-20% of total        Cabling, transformers, grid connection
- Engineering & Development 10-12% of total        Permits, design, project management
- Contingency               5-8% of total          Risk buffer for delays/cost overruns

OPERATIONAL EXPENDITURE (OPEX)
-------------------------------
Annual O&M Cost             $10 - $17 per kW/year  Including insurance and asset management
- Fixed O&M                 60-70% of OPEX         Panel cleaning, inverter maintenance
- Variable O&M              20-25% of OPEX         Component replacements
- Insurance                 8-12% of OPEX          Property and business interruption
- Asset Management Fee      3-5% of OPEX           Fund administration and reporting

PERFORMANCE METRICS
-------------------
Capacity Factor             17% - 22%              Location-dependent (solar irradiance)
Performance Ratio (PR)      82% - 88%              System efficiency (design & monitoring)
Annual Degradation Rate     0.4% - 0.6%            Panel performance decline per year
Panel Warranty Period       25 years               Performance guarantee typically 80% @ year 25
Inverter Lifespan           12-15 years            Major replacement required mid-life
Expected Project Life       30+ years              With proper maintenance and component replacement

ENERGY GENERATION (50MW Project Example)
----------------------------------------
Peak Capacity               50,000 kW
Annual Energy Output        87,600 - 96,360 MWh    Based on 2000-2200 hours full-load equivalent
Monthly Generation Pattern  Higher in summer months April-September typically 60% of annual
Grid Export Price           $0.08 - $0.15/kWh      Depends on PPA terms and market
Annual Revenue (Baseline)   $7.0M - $14.5M         Before O&M and debt service


2. FINANCIAL STRUCTURING BENCHMARKS
====================================

PROJECT IRR & RETURNS
---------------------
Project Equity IRR          8% - 12%               Pre-tax, depends on leverage
Levered IRR                 10% - 15%              With 60-70% debt financing
Unlevered IRR               7% - 10%               All-equity scenario
Token Holder Target Yield   6% - 9%                Annual distribution to token investors

DEBT FINANCING STRUCTURE
------------------------
Debt-to-Equity Ratio        60:40 to 70:30         Senior debt leverage range
Senior Debt Interest Rate   4.5% - 6.5%            Fixed rate project finance
Debt Tenor                  15 - 20 years          Matching PPA duration
Debt Service Coverage (DSCR) Min 1.25x             Typically 1.3x - 1.5x for investment grade
Loan-to-Value (LTV)         65% - 75%              At project completion

CASH FLOW WATERFALL (Typical Priority)
---------------------------------------
1. Operating Expenses       Fixed + Variable O&M
2. Senior Debt Service      Interest + Principal payments
3. Reserve Account Funding  Debt Service Reserve (6-12 months), Major Maintenance Reserve
4. Mezzanine Debt (if any)  Higher yield, subordinated
5. Equity/Token Distributions To investors after all obligations met
6. Performance Incentives   Management fees tied to performance

TOKENIZATION STRUCTURE
----------------------
Token Type                  Revenue Sharing Token / Asset-Backed Security Token
Minimum Investment          $10,000 - $100,000     Depends on accredited investor requirements
Token Valuation             Net Asset Value (NAV)  Quarterly or semi-annual revaluation
Distribution Frequency      Quarterly              Post cash flow stabilization (Year 2+)
Liquidity Options           Secondary market       Regulated trading platform or private transfer
Lock-up Period              12 - 24 months         Initial token holder restriction

4. HONG KONG REGULATORY FRAMEWORK FOR RWA
==========================================

SFC LICENSING REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------
Token Classification:       Likely "Security" under SFO if represents investment contract
Required Licenses:          Type 1 (Dealing in Securities) + Type 9 (Asset Management)
Exemptions Available:       Professional Investor-only offering (no retail authorization needed)
Minimum Capital:            HKD $5M (Type 1) + HKD $10M (Type 9) paid-up share capital
Responsible Officers:       Min 2 ROs with relevant experience (securities + fund management)

INVESTOR ELIGIBILITY
--------------------
Professional Investor (PI) Definition:
- Individual: Portfolio >HKD $8M or income >HKD $1M/year
- Corporation: Portfolio >HKD $8M or total assets >HKD $40M
- Institutional: Banks, insurers, pension funds (automatic PI status)

TOKEN OFFERING PROCESS
----------------------
Step 1: Legal Structure Setup      2-3 months (Cayman SPV + HK licensed entity)
Step 2: SFC License Application    6-12 months (if new entity) or 2-3 months (if existing licensee)
Step 3: Token Whitepaper Draft     1-2 months (legal + technical documentation)
Step 4: SFC Consultation           1-3 months (iterative feedback process)
Step 5: KYC/AML Infrastructure     1-2 months (onboarding system + compliance setup)
Step 6: Token Issuance             1 month (blockchain deployment + investor onboarding)
Total Timeline:                    12-18 months from inception to first token sale

KEY REGULATORY CONSIDERATIONS
------------------------------
1. Clear disclosure of risks (including smart contract risk, technology risk)
2. Custodian arrangement for underlying asset (solar project ownership documentation)
3. Valuation methodology (independent valuer for NAV calculation)
4. Ongoing reporting (quarterly financial + operational reports to token holders)
5. Cybersecurity and IT controls (for token platform and investor data)
6. Conflicts of interest management (if sponsor also asset manager)

COMPLIANCE COSTS (Estimated)
----------------------------
SFC License Application Fee:       HKD $18,580 (initial) + annual license fee
Legal & Compliance Setup:          $150,000 - $300,000 (one-time)
Annual Audit & Compliance:         $80,000 - $150,000 (recurring)
Fund Administration:               $50,000 - $100,000/year (NAV calculation + reporting)
Cybersecurity & IT Audit:          $30,000 - $60,000/year

6. RISK FACTORS & MITIGATION STRATEGIES
========================================

TECHNICAL RISKS
---------------
Risk: Panel degradation exceeds 0.6%/year
Mitigation: Tier 1 panel manufacturer with performance warranty, insurance claim mechanism
Financial Impact: -0.5% IRR per 0.1% additional degradation

Risk: Inverter failure before Year 12
Mitigation: Maintenance reserve fund ($500k), extended warranty from manufacturer
Financial Impact: -1.2% IRR if unplanned replacement in Year 8

REGULATORY RISKS
----------------
Risk: SFC imposes additional disclosure or capital requirements post-approval
Mitigation: Maintain compliance buffer (15% above minimum capital), legal counsel on retainer
Financial Impact: +$50k-$100k annual compliance cost

Risk: Change in renewable energy policy (subsidy or PPA pricing)
Mitigation: Lock in long-term PPA (15-20 years), diversify revenue with Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)
Financial Impact: Depends on specific policy change

MARKET RISKS
------------
Risk: Token secondary market liquidity insufficient
Mitigation: Market maker agreement, quarterly redemption window at NAV -2%
Financial Impact: Investor dissatisfaction but limited project cash flow impact

Risk: Interest rate rise increases refinancing cost (if short-term debt)
Mitigation: Fixed-rate project finance debt for full tenor
Financial Impact: Mitigated if fixed rate locked in
