   - Government co-funding: Up to 50% of installation cost for public chargers

MODULE 07: RISK & MITIGATION - EV CHARGING SPECIFIC
================================================================================

RISK MATRIX
-----------

CATEGORY 1: UTILIZATION RAMP RISK (CRITICAL)
--------------------------------------------
Risk: Charging stations achieve only 15-20% utilization vs projected 35-40%
Likelihood: Medium-High (50% probability of missing base case by 30%+)
Impact: High (Below-breakeven cash flow, unable to service debt)
Mitigation:
1. Pre-Launch Customer Acquisition: Partner with fleet operators (taxis, ride-hail, logistics) for guaranteed utilization
2. Site Selection Data: Use traffic data, EV registration heatmaps, competitor charger utilization
3. Anchor Tenant Model: Secure 2-3 large corporate customers (e.g., logistics company with 50 EV vans) for 30% baseline utilization
4. Phased Rollout: Deploy 6 stalls first, add next 6 only after first 6 achieve 30% utilization for 3 consecutive months
KPI: Weekly utilization % by site, cohort analysis (compare sites by location type)

CATEGORY 2: TECHNOLOGY OBSOLESCENCE
-----------------------------------
Risk: 180kW chargers become obsolete as 350kW+ "ultra-fast" chargers become standard
Likelihood: High (70% probability within 7 years)
Impact: Medium (Reduces competitiveness, but 180kW still usable for 70% of EVs)
Mitigation:
1. Modular Design: Choose chargers with swappable power modules (upgrade 180kW to 350kW for $30k vs $120k replacement)
2. Software-Defined: OCPP 2.0 protocol ensures chargers compatible with future payment/grid systems
3. Technology Escrow: Negotiate right to upgrade at cost with equipment supplier (Schneider, ABB)
4. Capex Reserve: $50k per site per 7 years for major technology refresh

CATEGORY 3: COMPETITIVE ENTRY (TESLA SUPERCHARGER OPENS TO ALL EVS)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Risk: Tesla opens Supercharger network to non-Tesla EVs, flooding market with 10,000+ chargers
Likelihood: Medium (40% probability, already happening in Europe/US)
Impact: High (Price competition, margin compression from $0.55/kWh to $0.40/kWh)
Mitigation:
1. Location Differentiation: Focus on destination charging (malls, office parks) vs highway corridors (Tesla's strength)
2. Value-Added Services: Offer valet charging, battery health monitoring, integration with building energy management
3. Membership Lock-in: Monthly subscription $200/month (unlimited charging) creates customer stickiness

CATEGORY 4: GRID CAPACITY CONSTRAINTS
-------------------------------------
Risk: Power utility unable to provide 2MVA transformer capacity, delaying site launch by 12+ months
Likelihood: Medium (30% in Hong Kong due to urban grid congestion)
Impact: Very High (Capex deployed but no revenue, interest accruing on debt)
Mitigation:
1. Pre-Qualified Sites: Only select sites where CLP/HKE has confirmed available capacity (add 3 months to site selection)
2. Behind-the-Meter Battery: Install 500kWh battery storage ($200k) to buffer peak demand, reduce transformer size requirement
3. Load Management: Intelligent load balancing across 6 stalls (never charge all 6 at 180kW simultaneously)
